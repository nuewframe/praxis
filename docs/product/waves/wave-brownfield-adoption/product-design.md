The user is a **team lead or principal engineer evaluating Praxis against a product that already ships**, and the agent operating on their behalf. The design question is not visual — it is what the adopter is asked to decide, in what order, and what they are shown about work the method never observed.

Two ambiguities worth resolving before authoring: how the adopter decides what counts as a coherent value theme in their own product (the derivation is a judgment call with no mechanical check), and what a delivered wave shows a reader who was not present for the work it describes.
